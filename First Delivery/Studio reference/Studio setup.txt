YOUR MAIL IS SCREAMING

MIDNIGHT PARCEL TERMINAL
Open the OPEN GAME shortcut in C:\Roblox and press F5. You arrive in the
station waiting room. Two couriers join the same numbered booth and read
the case notes. Both must be present to complete the house.

DEAD-LETTER HOUSE
The reader relays a witness statement from the ivory desk. The keyholder
matches its meaning to a seal. Roles reverse in the next room. Each room
draws a clue and gives the pair 105 seconds. A wrong answer wakes the
keeper, costs five seconds and requires the reader to relay the clue again.
The three rooms pay 15, 20 and 25 coins per courier. Closing the case
qualifies for the shared daily stamp. Classic Dispatch still offers the
original rooftop shift. Jump to stand up from a waiting-room bench.

LOBBY DESK
Invite opens Roblox's friend invitation prompt when the client supports it.
Daily grants 10, 15, 25, 35, 50, 75 and 120 post coins over seven claims.
One claim per UTC date. Missed dates preserve your place in the cycle.
Spin gives one free turn per UTC date. The eight equally likely wedges
pay 5, 10, 15, 5, 30, 10, 15 and 50 coins. A paid ticket buys another turn.
Shop sells coin packs. Troll sells a scare telegram; press Send to play
the surprise for everyone currently in the lobby.

ROBLOX PRODUCT SETUP
Studio runs offer labeled, free purchase demos. The live shop needs four
developer products owned by this published experience. In Creator
Dashboard > Monetization > Developer Products, create these products:
  Small   100 post coins        9 Robux
  Bundle  400 post coins       29 Robux
  Spin    1 wheel ticket        5 Robux
  Horn    1 scare telegram     35 Robux
Set each id in ReplicatedStorage > TerminalCatalog inside the place and
save/publish it. Dashboard prices determine the actual charge. The live
client requests Roblox's price for each product. Paid wheel purchases
and ticket use depend on the player's paid random item eligibility.
Use a capacity of 40 in the published place. Eight couriers can be
running four independent tours while others wait at the station.


CLASSIC SHIFTS
The Classic Dispatch booth leads to the original map, locker and jobs.
The maintenance bridge on the left keeps both parcels. The yellow OPEN HERE
pad spends one parcel to build your selected shortcut. A spring sends its
opener across immediately. Other couriers can ride it by using the pad's
prompt. Deliver at the red mailbox for 10 coins per remaining parcel.
When a teammate delivers after using your shortcut you earn 5 coins, once
for that teammate that shift, even if your receipt is already on screen.

The spring's squeal draws the Unclaimed up the fire escape. Get clear of
the yellow pad when the streetlight blinks. The creature takes one parcel
on contact and returns you to the last safe roof. With one parcel left you
can still finish. After it descends, the shared spring stays quiet.

THE DISPATCH BOARD
The teal board to the right of the counter opens before anyone picks up.
It shows your record, equipment and return-address clue. Choosing a route
changes the crew job. Your parcel type and wrapping apply to you. Choose
Done and pick up at the counter to begin. Each new shift defaults to Morrow
rooftops. Your published courier record remembers your equipment choices.

ROUTE CARDS AND THE MISPRINT OFFICE
Open Routes for your next punch. Record shows your whole card. Earn a
punch for handing in two parcels, delivering after riding a spring or
crossing a folding bridge, and delivering within 25 seconds of your own
pickup. A shift can qualify for several punches. Spare punches carry
forward. Each set pays 15 coins. Your title changes at 1, 3 and 6 cards.

Your first card unlocks the Misprint Office. Choose it under Routes.
Look at the destination on your box or HUD when you pick up. Cross to the
three sorting windows and stamp your manifest at Moon, Moth or Match.
Take the stamped parcel to the red mailbox for the usual pay plus 5 coins.

Spend earned coins under Locker. Firefly foil costs 45 and Midnight foil
costs 100. Buying equips a wrap, and you can switch owned wraps for free.

3 successful shifts     Pizza wrapping and Bellwether Mall
5 successful shifts     Quiet folding bridge (three seconds to unfold)
3 daily stamps          Airmail wrapping

One delivery counts as one successful shift, including solo runs. Mall
deliveries go through a shutter that opens for 3.5 seconds in every 10.
Bring both parcels to today's alternate mailbox within 30 seconds of the
first pickup for one stamp per UTC day. The mailbox moves on a three-day
rotation. Regular deliveries always count toward unlocks.

week to collect its dated postmark print. Record shows the newest four
prints and the total in your collection. Equip Collector's postmark in
Locker to wear your most recent print on your parcels.

There's one dead letter on each district's receiving roof. Search while
carrying a delivery. The board tracks the letters and reveals the final
job when you've read both. Choose that job, take the letter at dispatch,
and find the uniform beside the Dead Letter Office on the receiving roof.

SAVES
Local Studio play is a practice session. Stop resets the practice record.
In a published Roblox server, DataStoreService saves your coins, successful
shifts, collected letters, ending and stamps. Skin and equipment ownership
follow those milestones. Route punches, bought wraps and dated postmarks
also carry over in published servers. The board shows the current save state.
